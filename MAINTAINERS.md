## Implementer Liaisons

Implementer Liaisons are maintainers of registered Kindling implementations (other than Mycelial) who have shipped a working implementation. Liaisons can vote on RFCs and propose spec changes.

## How to become a maintainer

The path depends on the role.

**To become a Spec Reviewer:** Demonstrate sustained, high-quality engagement with the spec (reviewing RFCs, opening substantive issues, contributing accepted RFCs). Existing Spec Reviewers nominate new ones. Through v0.2, the Initial Maintainer signs off on invitations; from v0.3 onward, Spec Reviewers vote among themselves.

**To become an Implementer Liaison:** Ship a working implementation, list it on the registry, request Liaison status in a public discussion. Existing Spec Reviewers and Liaisons confirm.

**To become a Code of Conduct Committee member:** Existing committee members nominate. The Working Group confirms (per GOVERNANCE.md).

**To join the Working Group:** Follow the seat-specific selection process in GOVERNANCE.md. Most seats are elected by relevant constituencies (registered implementers, registered Pool curators, etc.).

---

## How to step down

Email `maintainers@kindling.dev` (for spec / reviewer roles) or `conduct@kindling.dev` (for committee roles) and let the rest of the team know. Stepping down is normal and accepted. We'd rather have engaged maintainers who choose to be here than tenured maintainers who don't have time anymore.

When you step down, your name moves to the **Past maintainers** section below with thanks.
